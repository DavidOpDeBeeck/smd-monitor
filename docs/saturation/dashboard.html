Receiving Fitting the specimens and the span index The tick

The hub fits its specimens into their allowance after every batch. At steady state it is always at the allowance, so each batch evicts hundreds of traces, and each eviction recomputes every application's bytes, builds a map of their usage, and picks the one furthest above its fair share before dropping a single trace. The span index forgets its entries the same way at every tick. Both cost evictions times applications, so the ceiling falls as services are added. The other large cost is rebuilding every key's statistics for each epoch.
